01 . The Challenge

Designing a believable fantasy character meant getting the right mix of realism and mystery. Modeling clean facial features and adding subtle details like tattoos and eye glow took a lot of fine-tuning.

Creating the robe with natural folds and a proper fabric look was tricky. Matching the desert lighting and making the scene feel cinematic without overcomplicating it was also a big part of the challenge.

02 . The Solution

I started with a simple base mesh and sculpted in the facial details, then used texture painting for the tattoos and skin. The robe was made using cloth simulation to get natural folds, and I added a fabric material to keep it realistic. For the staff, I used basic modeling and added a wood texture with bump maps. I lit the scene with soft HDRI lighting to match the desert feel and added slight color grading for a cinematic look.
